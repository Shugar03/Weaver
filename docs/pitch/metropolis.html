how trust works

Trust without trusting.

01 / MEASURED

Routing by measurement, not marketing

Every instance reports load and HOT/COLD state; the gateway measures real TTFT p50 and tok/s per request and ranks by ETR — weighted by on-chain ERC-8004 reputation (REP_WEIGHT). Calibration error is persisted and auditable per forge.

02 / VERIFIED

Delivery proof per job

Benchmark attestation on connect. Every result carries a secp256k1 personal_sign over the output hash bound to the exact dispatched prompt — including resume prefixes on failover. No signature, no payment.

03 / PAID

On-chain escrow, self-claim

WeaverEscrow on Monad: fundJob binds payment to the worker, release demands on-chain ecrecover of the proof. 24h self-claim window — the forge gets paid even if the operator vanishes. giveFeedback ERC-8004 posts {jobId, fundTx, releaseTx} after every settle.

the operator side

Why does a forge join?
Because the math closes.

A forge is any GPU that opts in: connects over an authenticated socket, passes attestation, serves jobs, gets paid in USDC per job — self-claimable within 24h even if the operator disappears. No protocol token. No rake on the job price.

  • earns the job price in USDC — proof-verified release, no counterparty risk on the operator
  • reputation compounds: ERC-8004 feedback weights the next route — REP_WEIGHT
  • early forges capture scarcity utilization — scarce supply routes them disproportionate share
  • image jobs are the yield: ~10× per GPU-hour vs text at today's API prices — diffusion pays, tokens are the floor
  • idleOnly: serve only when the machine is idle — it works while you sleep
